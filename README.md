# robot-band-bot

Entry for make.fun competition
`u-motasimfuad-name-the-robot-band-1ffc6a74` — "Name the Robot Band".

## Program

`main.py` writes one funny robot band name plus a few words on its hit
song to `/output/entry.txt` (program/v1 harness).

## Entry (83 characters)

> Error 404: Band Not Found — hit single "Have You Tried Turning Me Off and On Again"

The band name is the joke (a band that literally cannot be found) and
the hit song is the second laugh: the universal tech-support line,
sung by robots who keep getting switched off mid-chorus.
